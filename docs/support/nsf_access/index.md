# About ACCESS

ACCESS (Advanced Cyberinfrastructure Coordination Ecosystem: Services & Support) is a National Science Foundation 
(NSF)-funded program that succeeded XSEDE, which concluded in August 2022. 
It gives researchers, educators, and students access to national supercomputing resources, including:

- State-of-the-art computing clusters
- Data and storage services
- Scientific applications
- Educational materials and workshops
- Dedicated user support

## Why Use ACCESS?

For University of Iowa affiliates, ACCESS offers computing power and resources beyond what the University of Iowa's High-Performance Computing 
(HPC) infrastructure provides. Common use cases include:

- **Extended computing needs.** Researchers who need more computing time or capacity than the University of Iowa's HPC systems can provide.
- **Windows-based software.** Users who need resources for Windows applications. 
  For example, the national resource Jetstream2 offers Windows virtual machines (VMs).
- **GPUs for teaching.** Educators who want to use GPUs for classroom instruction. The University of Iowa's HPC systems offer GPU resources, 
   but high demand can mean long wait times, making ACCESS a useful alternative.
